Orders and quotes

All cakes and sweets are made to order. A quote confirms flavours, size, decoration and the pickup date. An order is confirmed once the quote is accepted and any agreed deposit is paid.

Changes and cancellations

Changes to confirmed orders depend on how close the request is to the pickup date and what has already been baked or purchased. Contact us as early as possible and we will do our best to accommodate.

Allergens

Products are made in a kitchen that handles dairy, eggs, gluten, nuts and soy. Tell us about allergies when ordering; traces may still be present.

Pickup and care

Once an order is picked up, transport and storage are the customer's responsibility. We will give simple care instructions with every order.
