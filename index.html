Certified home kitchen

Brazilian sweetness, baked in the Bay

Sra Receita is Eliana's bakery in Waitao, on the edge of Tauranga. The recipes come from Brazil. The berries, the butter and the customers come from the Bay of Plenty.

Everything is made to order in small batches, so your cake is baked for your date, not pulled from a freezer. Message with your occasion, flavours and numbers, and you'll get a clear quote and a pickup time.

  • Registered and certified home kitchen
  • Authentic Brazilian recipes, decorated by hand
  • Baked fresh to order for your date
E

Eliana

Founder and baker

The baker behind the bench

Sra Receita means "Mrs Recipe" in Portuguese, and Eliana is the recipe keeper. She bakes, decorates and packs every order herself, which is why the calendar fills up fast around the holidays.

Order early for big dates. For everything else, a few days' notice is usually all she needs.
