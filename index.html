FAQ

Good to know before you order

Can't see your question? Give Eliana a call or send an email.

How do I place an order?

Call or email with the date you need it, the occasion and roughly how many people. Eliana will talk you through flavours, sizes and decoration and confirm your order.

How far ahead should I order a custom cake?

The earlier the better, especially for weekends and themed cakes. Get in touch with your date and Eliana will confirm whether it's available.

What are brigadeiros and morango do amor?

Brigadeiros are Brazil's classic party sweet, a rich chocolate truffle rolled by hand. Morango do amor is a fresh strawberry wrapped in a creamy filling and coated in chocolate. Once you've tried them you'll understand the fuss.

Can you match my party theme?

Yes. Custom cakes are decorated to order, so share your theme, colours or a photo of the idea and Eliana will design the cake around it.

Where do I pick up my order?

Pickup is from Welcome Bay Road in Welcome Bay, Tauranga. You'll get the exact details when you order. Opening hours are in the contact section below.

Do you deliver?

Ask when you order. Depending on the day and where you are, Eliana will let you know what's possible.
